Nizamabad Tourism

Nizamabad is known for temples, historical monuments, lakes, and agricultural beauty.

Famous Tourist Places in Nizamabad
1. Nizamabad Fort
Historic fort built by Rashtrakutas
Panoramic city view
2. Dichpally Ramalayam
Ancient Rama temple
Known as “Khajuraho of Telangana”
3. Ali Sagar Reservoir
Beautiful lake and garden area
Boating and picnic spot
4. Pocharam Wildlife Sanctuary
Forest area with birds and wildlife
5. Mallaram Forest
Eco-tourism destination
Trekking and nature walks
