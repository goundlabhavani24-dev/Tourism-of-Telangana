
Adilabad is a beautiful district in northern Telangana known for waterfalls, forests, wildlife, and tribal culture. It is one of the best nature tourism destinations in the state.

Famous Tourist Places in Adilabad
1. Kuntala Waterfalls
Highest waterfall in Telangana
Located on Kadam River
Surrounded by dense forests
Best time: Monsoon and winter
2. Pochera Waterfalls
Scenic waterfall with deep water pools
Famous for photography and trekking
3. Kawal Wildlife Sanctuary
Tiger reserve and forest area
Home to tigers, deer, leopards, and birds
4. Basar Saraswati Temple
Famous temple dedicated to Goddess Saraswati
Popular for Aksharabhyasam ceremony for children
5. Jainath Temple
Ancient Lakshmi Narayana temple
Historic architecture
