Hyderabad is the capital city of Telangana.

Charminar is one of the most famous tourist attractions in Hyderabad. It was built in 1591 and is known for its historical architecture.

Golconda Fort is a historical fort famous for its acoustic system and beautiful city views.

Ramoji Film City is one of the largest film studio complexes in the world and attracts many tourists.

Hussain Sagar Lake is a popular lake with a large Buddha statue located in the middle.

Chowmahalla Palace was the residence of the Nizams and showcases royal architecture.

Salar Jung Museum contains historical artifacts, paintings, sculptures, and antique collections.

Birla Mandir is a white marble temple dedicated to Lord Venkateswara.

Hyderabad is also famous for Hyderabadi Biryani, Haleem, and Irani Chai.
